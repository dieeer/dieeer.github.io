Wrangle

Grade a video like a photo — one subject at a time.

Wrangle is a native iOS app for subject-aware video color grading. You pick a subject by tapping or lassoing it, and grade just that subject (or just the background) — exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation — while an on-device Track-Anything model keeps the selection on the subject as it moves. Everything runs on-device; footage never leaves the phone.

What it does

In-app library picker
Browse your videos with limited-access support and per-clip resolution badges, so you always know what you're grading.
Tap or lasso subject selection
Tap a subject or circle your own, and on-device segmentation turns it into a precise mask.
Local grading inside the mask
Adjust exposure, brightness, contrast, and saturation inside the mask — or invert it to grade everything but the subject.
Mask refinement & compare
Feather and edge controls refine the mask, with a live mask preview and a before/after compare.
Export back to Photos
Render the graded video and save it straight back to your Photos library.

Why it's different

Photo editors have had subject-aware adjustments for years — brush the sky, lift the shadows on a face. Video has mostly meant grading the whole frame, because the subject won't hold still.

Wrangle closes that gap. An on-device Track-Anything model follows your selection frame to frame, so a local grade sticks to a moving subject with no keyframing, no cloud, and nothing leaving the phone.
