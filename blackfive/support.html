Quick help

How do I play with friends?

Tap MULTIPLAYER, create a room, and share the 6-character code. Your friends choose JOIN and enter that code. The host starts the game once everyone's in.

A game stopped syncing.

Check your connection. The app reconnects automatically when your network returns; if a session can't recover, leave to the menu and start a new room.

How do I change or remove my handle?

Open the handle screen from the multiplayer menu to change it, or use the reset option in Settings to clear your local data.

How do I report an offensive handle or player?

Rooms are invite-only via your code. Leave the room and email abuse@justinre.id with details; we review and act on reports.
