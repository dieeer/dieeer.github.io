BLACK.FIVE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

BLACK.FIVE ("the app", "we", "us") is a real-time multiplayer card game. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as possible: no account, no email, no real name, and no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Information we collect

  • Player ID. When you first open the app it generates a random identifier (a UUID) stored only on your device. It is not derived from your hardware, phone number, email, or Apple ID, and it does not identify you personally.
  • Player handle (callsign). A short nickname you choose for multiplayer. You enter this yourself; you can leave it as the auto-generated default.
  • Multiplayer room data. When you create or join a room we store the room's 6-character code, its status, and the player IDs and handles of its members so the game can be played. Live gameplay state is exchanged between players in the session.
  • Local preferences. Settings such as audio, motion, and display options, stored on your device only.
  • Game Center (optional). If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple may authenticate you for gameplay features. That data is managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple ID.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for tracking, advertising IDs, or analytics/crash-reporting data via third-party SDKs.

2. How we use it

Solely to provide the game: creating and joining rooms, showing who is in a session, synchronising play in real time, and remembering your preferences. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or tracking across apps or websites.

3. Where it goes

Multiplayer room data (room code, player IDs, handles, game state) is sent to our backend provider, Supabase, which hosts the database and real-time service on our behalf as a data processor. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS) and is protected by server-side access rules so that rooms can only be read or modified through the game's own functions, using a room's exact code. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

4. Retention

Your Player ID, handle, and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Multiplayer rooms are transient: a room is removed when its host leaves or it becomes empty, and abandoned rooms are purged automatically. We do not keep a long-term history of your games.

5. Your choices & deletion

  • You can change or clear your handle at any time in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device (your Player ID, handle, and preferences).
  • To request deletion of any data associated with you from our backend, contact us at the address below and we will action it.

6. Children

BLACK.FIVE is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for ages 12+. It allows real-time play with other people who may be strangers, and players choose their own handles. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will remove it.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. Access to multiplayer data is restricted by server-side rules; the app ships only a public, restricted key and cannot read or alter arbitrary data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect the limited data we hold.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions or data requests: privacy@justinre.id.
