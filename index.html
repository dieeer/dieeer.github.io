How I work

I work closest to the product — design, engineering, and release, ideally none of them siloed. Give me a vague idea and I'll come back with something you can hold.

I've built in regulated environments where quality isn't optional, and on indie projects where shipping is. The thread through all of it is craft: the weight of a typeface, the feel of an interaction, the detail you only notice when it's missing. Small teams, clear scope, no waste.
