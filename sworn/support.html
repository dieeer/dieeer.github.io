Quick help

Does Sworn send my data anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics. The app does not access the internet.

Does the AI require an internet connection?

No. Sworn uses Apple Foundation Models which run entirely on your device. No data is sent to Apple or any third party for processing.

How do I connect my bank?

Open Banking is optional. Go to Settings and tap Connect Bank. You'll authorise the connection through your bank's app. Transactions are fetched and stored on your device only.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app. All data is stored locally and will be removed with the app. There is nothing on any server to delete.

What devices does Sworn support?

Sworn requires iOS 26 or later and a device with Apple Intelligence support for the on-device AI features.
