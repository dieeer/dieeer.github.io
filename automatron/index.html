automatron

Turn your Spotify listening history into a wallpaper that updates itself.

Siri Shortcut

Generate Wallpaper

Creates a mosaic wallpaper from your recent Spotify album art using your in-app modifiers — timescale, filters, grid size — and saves it straight to Photos.

Open in Shortcuts Open Shortcuts App

Set up automation

1

Install automatron

The app registers a native "Generate Wallpaper" action in Shortcuts automatically.

2

Create an automation

Open Shortcuts, tap Automation, pick a trigger — time of day, charger connected, or a Focus mode.

3

Add the action

Search for "automatron" and add the "Generate Wallpaper" action to your automation.

4

Set your wallpaper

Find the latest image in your "automatron" album in Photos. Pair with a Focus-linked wallpaper for fully hands-free switching.

*

The shortcut uses whatever modifiers you've configured in the app — timescale, genre filters, grid columns, and watermark. Change them any time in Settings.
