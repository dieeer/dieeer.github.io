Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Overview

automatron is an iOS app that generates wallpapers from your Spotify listening history. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and what we don't do.

Data we access

  • Spotify recently played tracks — we request the user-read-recently-played scope via Spotify's OAuth (PKCE) flow. This gives us your recent listening history so we can fetch album artwork.
  • Photo library (add-only) — we save generated wallpapers to a dedicated "automatron" album in your Photos. We request add-only access and never read or browse your existing photos.

Data we store

  • Spotify access and refresh tokens — stored locally on your device in UserDefaults to keep you signed in. These never leave your device.
  • Your modifier preferences — timescale, filters, grid size, and watermark settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Last generated wallpaper — cached locally on your device for display on relaunch.

Data we don't collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDK, and no third-party data sharing. All processing happens entirely on your device.

Spotify

automatron authenticates with Spotify using the Authorization Code with PKCE flow. We only request the minimum scope needed (user-read-recently-played). You can revoke access at any time from your Spotify account settings.

Subscriptions

automatron offers an optional premium subscription managed entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not process or have access to any payment information.

Children

automatron is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page with a new "last updated" date.
