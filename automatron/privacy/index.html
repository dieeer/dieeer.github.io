Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What Automatron does

Automatron connects to your Spotify account to read your recently played tracks and uses the album artwork to generate a personalised wallpaper for your device.

Data we access

  • Spotify access token — stored locally on your device only. Never sent to our servers.
  • Recently played tracks — fetched directly from Spotify's API on your device. We do not store, log, or transmit this data.
  • Album artwork images — downloaded from Spotify's CDN and rendered locally into your wallpaper image.

Data we do not collect

Automatron has no backend server. We do not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or any personally identifiable information. Your Spotify credentials are handled entirely by Spotify's own OAuth flow.

Third-party services

Automatron uses Spotify's Web API to fetch your listening history. Your use of Spotify is governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit. No payment information passes through Automatron.

Permissions

  • Photos — used only to save generated wallpapers to your photo library. We do not read your existing photos.

Children

Automatron is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance.
