Spotify won't connect

Make sure you have a Spotify account (free or premium). Tap "Connect Spotify" and log in through the browser that appears. If it fails, try disconnecting and reconnecting from Settings.

The wallpaper doesn't fill my screen

Automatron generates at your device's native resolution. If it appears letterboxed, make sure you set it as wallpaper with "Fill" or "Scale to Fill" in the iOS wallpaper picker.

I'm not seeing recent music

Spotify's recently-played API requires you to have listened to tracks while online. Offline plays may not appear immediately. Try generating again after a fresh online session.

Restore my Premium purchase

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases inside the app. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Cancel subscription

Open the Settings app on your device → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Automatron Premium → Cancel.
